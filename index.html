Group Books Review

A private QuickBooks Online tool. Not offered to the public.

Group Books Review is an internal tool used by its owner to read and reconcile the QuickBooks Online books of companies he owns or manages, and to post journal entries he has approved. It runs only on the owner's own computer.

It is not sold, listed in any app store, or available to other QuickBooks users.

Connected

If you reached this page after connecting a company: the connection is complete. You can close this window and return to the tool.

Disconnecting

To disconnect, open QuickBooks Online → Apps → Manage apps and disconnect "Group Books Review - private". The tool's own disconnect command does the same. Once disconnected, the tool can no longer access that company.
